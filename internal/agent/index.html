01 / The system

From individual agents
to a managed workforce.

More agents do not automatically mean more useful work. Human Minority turns individual capabilities into organised execution: clear responsibilities, coordinated tasks and results that can be evaluated.

01

Manage

Define goals, priorities, roles and responsibilities. Organise work around human objectives.

02

Coordinate

Assign work, manage dependencies and run multi-agent workflows.

03

Control

Enforce permissions, execution boundaries, budgets and resource limits.

04

Verify

Separate production from acceptance. Require evidence and independent checks.

05

Scale

Increase the volume and diversity of work one person can direct.

02 / Operating model

You set the direction.
The system organises work.

Human Minority is designed as the management layer for the entire workflow. The human defines goals and authority boundaries. The system organises execution and prepares results for human decision.

01

Direct

Set the outcome, priorities and boundaries.

02

Execute

Agents work within their assigned responsibilities and resources.

03

Verify

Assess the results independently against the required evidence.

04

Decide

Review results, unresolved risks and decisions that require human authority.

03 / Human authority

More agents.
Same veto.

Power scales. Authority stays human. Control and verification are foundations of the management system, not substitutes for it.

Capability ≠ trust.

Capability alone does not grant permission.

Claim ≠ proof.

An agent’s report is not proof that the work is correct.

Reputation ≠ authority.

Past performance does not override human authority.

04 / Application

Work is not limited
to software.

Software engineering is the first application. The system is intended for agent-assisted work wherever tasks, responsibilities, resources and acceptance criteria can be defined.

Software engineering

Implementation, testing, review, documentation and release workflows.

Research

Information gathering, analysis, experimentation and evaluation.

Business operations

Analysis, planning, reporting and coordinated operational workflows.

Illustrative application areas; not a claim of currently available integrations or domain-specific validation.
